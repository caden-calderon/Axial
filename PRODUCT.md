# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Axial serves new and returning strategy-game players on desktop and mobile, including friends playing private online matches, solo players facing the AI, and experienced players exploring deeper board and rule variants. They may arrive with no knowledge of the original project or of three-dimensional gravity games. Their primary job is to understand the board quickly, choose a match with minimal friction, place pieces confidently, read threats and completed lines, and recover or rematch without losing context.

## Product Purpose

Axial is a polished browser-native 3D gravity strategy game: familiar enough to explain as Connect 4 in three dimensions, but spatial and expressive enough to develop its own identity. The product succeeds when a first-time player can begin without confusion, the active board state remains readable under pressure, setup complexity stays out of the way during play, and local, AI, and private-online matches feel equally intentional on desktop and mobile.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, tactical, welcoming. Axial should feel like a precise physical game table from a near-future world: atmospheric and distinctive, but never cryptic, noisy, or self-consciously futuristic. Its voice is concise, confident, and encouraging.

## Anti-references

- Generic neon sci-fi dashboards whose chrome competes with the content.
- Casino-like or hyperactive mobile-game interfaces that turn every state into a reward effect.
- Opaque WebGL art demos that expect users to discover basic controls by accident.
- Dense settings consoles that make configuration feel more important than the match.
- Long instructional overlays that explain the game without helping users learn by doing.

## Design Principles

1. **The board is the hero.** Interface chrome supports spatial play and yields visual priority to the board.
2. **Teach through confident action.** Use progressive disclosure, useful defaults, previews, and contextual guidance so players learn while playing.
3. **Make every state legible.** Turn ownership, legal intent, locked settings, online presence, errors, wins, and recovery states should be clear without relying on color alone.
4. **Let motion explain the game.** Animation should communicate placement, causality, completed lines, transitions, and network state while remaining fast and fully reduced-motion compatible.
5. **Treat mobile as a native play surface.** Preserve board readability, thumb reach, safe areas, interruption recovery, and touch-target comfort rather than shrinking the desktop UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the application chrome, onboarding, setup, and match overlays. Support visible keyboard focus, meaningful labels and live-region updates, at least 44 by 44 CSS-pixel primary touch targets, browser zoom and text scaling, reduced motion, and state cues that combine color with text, shape, or iconography. The 3D board should retain a clear confirm-drop path for touch and motor accessibility, with future keyboard and non-visual play affordances treated as first-class product work rather than hidden implementation details.
