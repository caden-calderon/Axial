# Axial overview video: script (draft 6, 2026-10-20)

Caden's notes on cut 1: the tutorial card as the intro is lazy, and the cut straight after "This is Axial"
is bad. The intro should say what the game is, then how you play it, then how to set it up, then where
to play it. Spine (Caden): "This is Axial. Think Connect 4, but in 3D, with a few twists. Play local,
online or against an AI. Adjust settings and game rules. Try it out today."

Caden's notes on cut 2: much better, but some parts need more time to breathe so the text can be read,
and it should be more energetic, with more creative editing. A fresh-context review agreed. Its main
points:

- Lines were up for less time than they take to read: the twist pills, "Four in a row…", "Scroll to
  zoom", "It plays you back", and the end card.
- The middle 21 s was panel clicks over empty boards.
- Every shot ran 2-6 s, so there was no rhythm.

Draft 4 answers these:

- Freeze frames on each win line add reading time without slowing the game down.
- Speed ramps go through the slow stretches: the board growing, the panel collapsing, the orbit.
- A burst of short cuts on landings sits under "…with a few twists."
- Slam titles, one per twist.
- Zoom-through cuts at section changes, and a small zoom pulse on every impact.
- Controls are shown on a match already under way.
- A side-by-side desktop and phone scene.
- A 5 s end card.

Why cut 1's intro cut was bad: the welcome take ends on the dimmed board as the tour card closes, then
hard-cuts to the controls take from a different camera angle with a 1.18 punch-in already applied. The
angle, the zoom and the brightness all jump at once, and nothing is moving across the cut. Every cut now
lands on an action (a piece landing, a click), a matching camera move, or a zoom-through.

Caden's notes on cut 3, and what changed:

- The UI pill showed at the top. Punch-ins cropped the game's top bar halfway. The rule now: the top
  bar is either whole or cropped away entirely (`hideChrome`).
- Bigger boards and "adjust the board" said the same thing, as did twists and rules. The twists are
  now the settings: each change is cut on its click to a board where it's in play.
- White flashes and weird cuts. The freeze-frame flash and the zoom-through cut are gone; cuts are
  straight, on an action.
- A bad cut at 13-14 s. The big-board shot ran into the result card. Win shots now end on their
  freeze frame, and the edit refuses a win shot that runs past the result card (1.76 s after the
  winning move).
- "It blocks your threats" was dumb. It's replaced by Max winning a match under way, with only the
  game's own "AI wins" chip.

Caden's notes on cut 4 (2026-10-20), and what changed (cut 5):

- A weird cut at 11-12 s as the menu closes. The 9×9×9 shot opened while that take's own settings panel
  was still sliding shut. The take now closes it off camera, and the shot shows the whole board turning
  slowly for about 2.2 s under "Bigger boards."
- Longer lines: after the drop, turn to show it's five in a row. The camera now swings side-on to the
  staircase straight after the winning drop, before the result card, and the freeze holds that view.
- The Max-wins scene at ~40 s was pointless. Cut; the levels shot holds Max selected for 0.35 s, then
  cuts to desktop and phone.
- End card: only the A of AXIAL was coloured. The wordmark is now one colour, as in the game, with the
  accent shining across it once.

Caden's notes on cut 5, and what changed (cut 6, 53 s):

- The Lines to win click was too quick to see. The panel shot now runs 1.55 s (the pointer arriving, 3
  lighting up); the Connect 5 shot got the same treatment (1.1 s).
- The levels at ~39 s needed to breathe: they play at real speed (only the opponent switch is 1.25×),
  and Max holds 0.8 s.
- The end card's shine was too fast: it now crosses the wordmark over 3 s.
- The dark halos behind the titles (a scrim and a text shadow) looked weird and are gone, as is the

- A little more time on the Connect 5 (1.8 s) and Lines to win (2.0 s) clicks.
- Music: Caden's ready to pick. Shortlist sent 2026-10-20.
