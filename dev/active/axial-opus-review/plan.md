# Axial Opus review: plan

Status: complete. 29 implementation commits plus 4 fixes from the Codex review (see `tasks.md` and `context.md`). Two read-only audits ran in parallel (experience: `apps/web`; engine: core, AI, worker transport, rooms, tests). Their full reports are summarised in `audit-experience.md` and `audit-engine.md`.

## Approach

1. Baseline every gate at `793e0db` before changing anything (see `context.md`).
2. Audit in parallel, read-only, with file:line evidence, then triage here: what ships, what is deferred, and what is a proposal for Caden.
3. One focused commit per change. Check, lint, unit tests and build after each meaningful change; the relevant Playwright specs for UI-affecting changes.
4. AI changes ship only with before/after evidence from the evaluation suite or the paired head-to-head harness against the frozen 793e0db bundle. A change that weakens the AI or slows the UI does not ship.
5. Independent Codex review (read-only) over the cumulative diff against `793e0db`, then fixes and a final gate.

## Boundaries

Keep the rules, win conditions, visual language and Caden's approved September UI. Rule changes and large visual redesigns are proposals, not implementations. No new runtime dependencies without a strong reason. Untouched: `axial-video/`, the article files in `dev/active/axial-announcement-readiness/`, `Reports/`, `docs/papers/`, `axial-unity/`.

## Triage: engine audit (B)

Agent B used an exact AND-OR proof search (`/tmp/axial-opus-review/engine/scripts/oracle.mjs`) to classify moves as safe or lost within N plies, and prototyped fixes in scratch copies of the source. Wall-clock numbers were taken on a loaded machine.

| ID  | Sev | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --- | --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| B1  | P1  | The fork-block shortcut (`block-forcing`) returns before lookahead or MCTS run, and only considers the opponent's fork cells. Hard and Max play moves their own depth-3 lookahead scores as lost (−9.8M), e.g. `[[3,3],[2,4],[3,1]]` → (3,2), lost in 5 plies; this is Max's loss to Hard in `eval:strength:hard`. | **Ship**: presets with lookahead depth ≥ 2 send fork blocks to search instead of returning early; Easy/Medium keep the shortcut. ## Deferred and proposals for Caden

- **A2 Reset safety**: confirm step, undo toast, or Undo in the collapsed phone toolbar (with A1's review bar shipped, this is the remaining mobile risk).
- **A11 Shine once per turn** instead of the endless sweep (≈155 repaints/s idle).
- **A15 One position vocabulary** (X/Y/Z vs row/column/layer vs H/R/C).
- **A21 Minimum label size** of 11 px desktop / 12 px phone.
- **A22 Audio**: master gain/compressor and a soft line-scored chime for multi-line matches; needs a listening check.
- **Portrait camera** (A): ≈40° elevation and scale ≈0.76 grows phone cell area ≈69%; changes the approved portrait framing.
- **Instanced pieces** (A5 follow-up): ≈8 draw calls instead of ≈320 on dense boards.
- **Theme colour and orientation**: light theme keeps the dark `theme-color`; the manifest locks installed tablets to portrait.
- **Max budget cap** (B): Max reaches ≈13 s per move on 10³ three-line; a Connect-5 three-line game is ≈12 minutes of AI thinking.
- **Medium veto** (B): Medium's own lookahead finds the safe move in B's R3 but Medium plays the anchor; applying the proven-loss veto to Medium makes it stronger, i.e. changes its identity.
- **Line-merge rule** (B): on boards with a dimension ≥ 9 in Connect 4, filling the gap between two scored runs merges them into one line, so a player's own line count can drop. Decide whether scored lines are permanent.
- **First-player advantage** (B): after P1 opens (3,3) only 2 of 42 replies survive an exact 7-ply check; a threat-space search would be the next real strength lever.
- **Easy identity** (B): Easy is close to random (12–55% 3-ply-losing picks in trap positions); consider a deliberately human-like Easy.
- **A20 Error recovery scope**, B10, B11: deferred with reasons above.

### Deferred engineering (not proposals, just not done in this pass)

- **A5 shared piece geometries / instancing.** Step 1 (module-level geometries) saves memory and mount time but no draw calls; instancing is the real win (≈320 → ≈8 draw calls on dense boards) and is a larger refactor of GamePiece's per-piece animation. With A3 the board no longer renders while idle, so dense-board cost now only applies during animations.
- **B3 lookahead iteration budgeting.** Real waste (45% of lookahead time), but B4 made nodes 1.5–3.7× faster, which changes the profile, and judging a budgeting change needs many paired games; first-player wins dominate engine-vs-engine games (P1 won 36 of 40), so those games barely separate engines. Needs a fixture/oracle-based evaluation first.
- **Compact landscape confirm bar** (A17): the bar covers the front rows on 844×390; a right-aligned compact variant needs a design pass.
- **Fork-block latency** (B1 follow-up): Hard and Max now spend their full budget when an instant fork block is provably lost (trap-position p95: Hard 0.06–0.14 s → 1.4–2.4 s, Max 0.05–0.13 s → 3.6–6.1 s), including positions that are already lost. Typical median latency is unchanged in practice (16 → 67 ms, under the UI's 650 ms minimum turn). If Caden prefers the old instant replies, the check is one guarded branch in `mcts.ts` (`refutedForkBlock`).
