2. [AI and MCTS](02-Axial-AI-and-MCTS.pdf), 14 pages. SHA-256: `c6c1d80133dc8abcd3bf67ebe41a852cdb7be7faf5d8799ae619f3f00b855a72`.

The AI PDF's Enhanced-versus-Basic MCTS table reports 20 wins out of 20. The checked-in [AI LaTeX source](../../archive/original-project/reports/AI_Report.tex) instead reports 15 wins out of 20. The underlying experiment has not been reproduced, so neither result should be treated as independently verified. The game PDF also retains a sentence that extends into the right margin on page 8. Both reports remain unaltered to preserve their original record.
